# Concept frames for the remodel (spec 2026-11-17 fun first, phase F0)

Three art directions for the same Night, then a Day frame in the direction the owner picks. Generated by the owner;
the results go in this folder as `night-a-toy.png`, `night-b-painterly.png`, `night-c-lowpoly.png` and `day-<x>.png`.

Each prompt is the shared scene plus one style block. Generate portrait (9:16, or 1080 × 1920 or larger) with no UI and
no text in the image.

## The scene (same in all three)

> A mobile tower-defence game screenshot, portrait 9:16, camera high above at a 55° angle looking down, close enough
> that the characters are big and readable. Night, in a muddy medieval borderland valley around the year 950.
>
> In the centre, a small comfy spa-cult village built from junk: a ruined Roman bath with white marble columns and a
> steaming warm pool, a Slavic log sauna bolted onto it with a stone chimney, a bright blue plastic tarp roof, a yellow
> rubber duck statue on top, and a glowing campfire hearth casting a big warm orange pool of light. Around it: a thatched
> cottage, a wooden woodcutter's lean-to, a wattle hopper on a plank ramp (a slurry chute), and a wooden catapult tower
> that flings saplings.
>
> A dark muddy dirt road winds in from the top of the frame to the village, lit by the raiders' flaming torches. On it,
> raiders in dark red and gold: hooded peasant levies with pitchforks, a soldier with a pointed nasal helmet and a kite
> shield, and a tonsured monk throwing a hymn book.
>
> In front of the village, the hero: a tired, slightly chubby man from 2024, a head taller than the locals, in a white
> bathrobe over a t-shirt, neon-pink flip-flops, sunglasses pushed up on his head, a belt bag, swinging a black umbrella
> at two levies who have left the road to chase him. A glowing teal ring on the ground under his feet. Big friendly
> chibi proportions, head about half the body height, round faces, expressive eyebrows, deadpan humour.
>
> Anachronistic 2024 junk scattered around in loud neon: orange traffic cones, a hi-vis vest drying on a fence, a
> Bluetooth speaker, a car seat. Earthy desaturated browns and greens for the medieval world; the 2024 junk always in
> neon. Cool teal moonlight against warm orange fire. Pine forest and fog at the edges, mountains beyond. Clear
> silhouettes, every character instantly readable on a phone screen.

## A. Chunky toy diorama

> Style: a polished 3D toy diorama, like a premium mobile strategy game. Chunky rounded shapes with soft bevelled edges,
> hand-painted textures on wood, stone and thatch, soft ambient occlusion, a strong warm rim light on every character,
> slight tilt-shift depth of field. Glossy but not plastic. Rich, warm, cosy. Render quality of a high-end 3D mobile game
> key art.

## B. Storybook painterly

> Style: a hand-painted storybook illustration rendered as a game scene. Soft painterly brushwork, gentle colour
> gradients, thin dark ink outlines around characters and buildings, visible paper texture, glowing warm lamplight and
> cool blue shadows, like a beautifully illustrated children's book come to life. Characters stay crisp and readable
> against the painted ground.

## C. Crisp stylised low-poly

> Style: a clean, crisp stylised low-poly 3D game in the style of top mobile kingdom-builder and tower-defence games.
> Simple faceted shapes with flat colours and smooth gradient lighting, bold silhouettes, a strong colour contrast
> between the red-and-gold raiders and the white-and-neon cult, minimal texture, very clean and readable, bright
> highlights and deep soft shadows.

## The Day frame (after the pick)

Same style block as the chosen direction, with this scene instead:

> A mobile base-building game screenshot, portrait 9:16, camera high above at a 55° angle, warm late-afternoon golden

> steaming pool, the log sauna and its blue tarp, the rubber duck statue, three thatched cottages, a woodcutter's

> cosy, alive, every building clearly distinct.

## The chosen direction: toy diorama with ink outlines (owner, 2026-11-17)

A (the chunky toy diorama) as the base, with B's dark cartoon outlines and C's readability: saturated team colours, a
clean ground where the fight is, torches along the road. Two full prompts, self-contained: a Night and a Day.
Results: `night-final.png`, `day-final.png`.

## Round 2 (owner, 2026-11-17): the car, the cargo-cult rule, one style

Round 1 (`night-final-v1.jpg`, `day-final-v1.jpg`) left out the heart of the setting: the teal hatchback time machine,
nose-down in the Roman pool (GDD 05). It also broke the cargo-cult rule (cones and tarps everywhere, plastic crates),
put a campfire where the Hearth should be, and drew the Night as a flat 2D cartoon and the Day as a 3D diorama. Round 2
fixes all four. Results: `night-final.png`, `day-final.png`.
