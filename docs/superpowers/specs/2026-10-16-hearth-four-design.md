# Hearth 4: the Comfort Era begins

- **Date:** 2026-10-16
- **Status:** Built while the owner was away ("keep building, then build something else, new, next levels or something"). Numbers are first guesses, checked with the pace bot.

Hearth 3 was the top. A village that reached it in about 7 hours had nothing new to aim for. Hearth 4 opens the Comfort Era of GDD 03: a new tower, three new buildings, four studies and five errands. The new buildings use models that were made but never used (sauna, blacksmith, beehive); the tower gets a model of its own.

## New buildings

| | Opens | Size | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Beehive** | Hearth 2 | 1×1 | 60 T | Devotion 4/h and Comfort +2. Nobody works it: bees. Up to 3 (5 at Hearth 4). |
| **Sauna** | Hearth 4 | 2×2 | 1,200 T · 200 P · 100 S | Comfort +25, Devotion 12/h; burns 6 Peat/h. 1 worker. |
| **Blacksmith** | Hearth 4 | 2×2 | 1,500 T · 300 S | Scrap 70/h, Comfort −5 (the noise). 2 workers. |
| **Pressure Sluice** (tower) | Hearth 4 | 1×1 | 150 T · 150 S | Hits one raider (14) and knocks it 1½ tiles back up the road, every 3 s. Range 3. |

The Pressure Sluice is the GDD's Comfort-era tower: a raised barrel, a leather hose and a brass nozzle. It pushes raiders back into the Chute's mud and the Flinger's reach, and it washes a swinging raider off the Prophet.

## Errands (after "Win a Night with 2 stars")

1. **Build a Beehive.**
2. **Raise the Hearth to level 4.**
3. **Build a Sauna.**
4. **Put a Pressure Sluice beside the raiders' road.**
5. **Build a Blacksmith.**

Errands are saved by id, so appending them moves nobody.
