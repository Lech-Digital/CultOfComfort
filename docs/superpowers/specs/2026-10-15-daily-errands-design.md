# The Prophet's daily list

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Status:** Approved by the owner: "daily errands. after we finish the onboarding initial tasks, we should use those for daily missions". He picked "Towels + cosmetics" and a "Local 04:00" reset.

## The list

- **When it starts:** once the 16 starter errands are done, the errand note becomes the day's list.
- **When it resets:** a day starts at **04:00 on the player's clock**. The phone sends its UTC offset when it loads (`SetDayClock`). The day never goes back.
- **What's on it:** **three errands a day**, the same three for every cult on that day, drawn by shuffling the pool by the day. A study is skipped when there's nothing left to study.

| Errand | Counts | Target (Hearth 1–2) | Reward (Hearth 1–2) |
|--------|--------|----------------------|---------------------|
| preach | hours sent out preaching | 4 | 25 Devotion |
| calm | demands delivered + tithes paid | 1 | 120 Timber |
| win-night | Nights won with ★★ or more | 1 | 50 Scrap |
| night | Nights seen through | 1 | 100 Timber, 30 Peat |
| offerings | offerings collected (tithe value) | 150 | 20 Devotion |
| upgrade | upgrades started | 1 | 150 Timber |
| repair | repairs | 1 | 40 Scrap |
| roads | road tiles laid | 5 | 80 Timber |
| study | studies finished | 1 | 30 Devotion |
| trade | Market deals | 1 | 60 Peat |
| road-find | preachers home with a road find | 1 | 20 Devotion |
| build | new buildings (not roads) | 2 | 120 Timber |

**Scaling:** at Hearth 3, counted targets and rewards grow ×1.5; from Hearth 4, ×2. A reward never overfills the stores.

## Thermos, streak and Towels

- **The Thermos:** once all three errands are claimed, it pays 30 Devotion and **3 Towels**.
- **The streak:** it counts Thermoses on consecutive days, and a day without one breaks it. Every **7th** Thermos in a row pays **10 Towels** and the next cosmetic, in this order: golden duck, woad bunting, prophet's sunhat, towel flags, garden gnome. The golden duck already shows by the Hearth; the others are to come.
- **What Towels buy:**

| Spend | Cost | Effect |
|-------|------|--------|
| Hurry | 1 | 30 minutes off the construction under way (on its card) |
| Swap | 2 | A different errand in an unclaimed slot, with its progress reset |

## Pace

- **Errand rewards:** about 30–45 minutes of a Hearth 2–3 village's production each.
- **Towels:** they don't buy resources. They buy convenience, and a little time back.
- **Pace bots:** unchanged, because the starter errands come first.
