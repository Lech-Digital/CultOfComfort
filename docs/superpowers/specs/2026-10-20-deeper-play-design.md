# Deeper play: everything from the engagement list, and what Kingshot suggested

- **Date:** 2026-10-20
- **Status:** The owner approved all of it ("all the ideas are great. lets implement them"), adding: longer builds ("at higher levels, take days even"), the coach shouldn't ask for a raid while shielded, wrecks should look broken, better models, and "open Kingshot on my phone … get inspired".

## Fixes and pacing

- **The coach and the shield.** While shielded, the coach no longer points at Lamps that can't be lit. It says "Shielded for now. Get the defences ready."
- **Long builds:**
  - **Hearth:** 6 hours to level 3, a day to level 4, three days to level 5.
  - **Buildings:** top levels take 4 hours to 2 days; first levels stay quick.
- **Builders:** 1, 1, 2, 2, 3 by Hearth level.
- **Towels:** a Towel takes an hour off the job whose card you tap, and every errand pays a Towel.

## Wrecks look wrecked

- **Gates and towers** switch to broken models.
- **Other buildings** are knocked askew, half flattened and charred, on a heap of rubble.
- **Smoke** rises from every wreck.

## Hands-on Nights: dusk tricks

Both tricks are laid at dusk, used up by the Night, and carried in the plan.
- **Mud Trap:** goes on the raiders' road. It sticks the first three raiders who step in it: slowed hard, and muddied, so towers crit.
- **Decoy Duck:** every raider within three tiles that smashes things goes for it first.

## Short daily goals: visitors

From Hearth 2, someone turns up in each four-hour slot on the clock and waits two or three hours:
- a pilgrim (a bath, then they stay as a villager);
- a pedlar (Timber for Scrap, Scrap for Towels…);
- a lost sheep to send home.

Slots on the clock mean every device sees the same visitor; the save remembers only which slot was answered.

## The Prophet grows

- **Experience:** 20 for every Night plus 25 a star.
- **Levels:** ten. Each adds 4% health and 5% damage.
- **Perks:** at levels 3, 5, 7 and 9 he picks one of two (bathrobe or slippers, megaphone or hotter stones, …).
- **Where it shows:** tap the portrait for his sheet; a red dot means a perk is waiting.
- **Replays:** all of it is in the Night plan, so a level-1 Prophet without perks fights exactly as before.

## Notables

Two named villagers join at each Hearth level from 2. Each is good at something:
- Agnes the Axe: +40% Timber;
- Sister Ember: works a tower, so it fires faster;
- and others.

A building's card asks the best idle one to work there; the People tab lists them.

## Seasons

Three days each on the clock:

| Season | Nights | The valley |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | The south bridge floods: nobody comes that way. | Fresh green, rain at Night |
| Summer | Raids 15% smaller. | Fireflies at Night |
| Autumn | Raids 15% bigger, and half as much Scrap again after. | Russet, falling leaves |
| Winter | Raiders 15% slower. | Snow, the river iced |

- The first Night keeps its fixed size.
- The season shows under the portrait and is announced when it turns.

## New buildings (Hearth 3)

- **Watchtower:** the next two Nights, who and which road.
- **Tavern:** Devotion and Comfort; villagers arrive in 60% of the time.
- **Bell Tower:** cultists and wardens hit half as hard again at Night.

## Better models

- The Hearth at level 5: a golden dome with the gilded Duck Ascendant, standing stones, banners.
- Towers kick when they fire.
- New models: the mud trap, the decoy duck, the watchtower, the tavern, the bell tower, rubble, and the broken gate and towers.

## From Kingshot

- **The Pilgrims' Way** (its Conquest):
  - an endless ladder of challenge Nights, any time, shield or no shield, wrecking nothing;
  - a boss every fifth stage;
  - each stage cleared pays;
  - an alms box fills by the highest stage cleared, for up to 12 hours.
- **Welcome back:** what the stores gained, who moved in, what finished, what's waiting.
- **The Comfort Festival** (its weekly events): a theme a day, three reward tiers.

## Not done

- A Ferry: a crossing you control would need a lot of new road rules.
- Raiders that counter specific defences (boars charging past gates, the Bishop blessing).
- An alliance or any social layer.
