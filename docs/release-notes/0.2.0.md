# Cult of Comfort 0.2.0 (version code 2)

The "fun first" release: the plan from the playtest of 0.1.0 (spec
[`2026-11-17-fun-first-design.md`](../superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-fun-first-design.md)), phases A to E, and the first
step of the remodel (F1). Branch `playtest-and-redesign`.

## What's new on Google Play (en-US, 500 characters at most)

```
A Night that needs you: waves come with a breather between them, pick a boon or call the next wave early for more Scrap, and earn your stars with the Night's objective.
```

## Everything in this release

### A Night you can read
- The camera comes in on the fight, follows the Prophet with a dead zone, and eases back after a pinch. The spare room
  goes up the road the raiders come down, not behind the Hearth.
- The Prophet is found at a glance: a glowing teal ring, a dark outline, a little taller at Night.
- Ranges are drawn: the umbrella's reach solid, the lure's dashed, Hot Stones' throw whenever it's hot. A tap just
  outside a range turns into the order you meant.
- Bigger raider health bars; red arrows at the screen's edge for raiders out of sight; a red pulse with a "!" when a
  building or the Hearth is hit out of sight; the Hearth's meter orange, the Prophet's teal.
- The skill panel: round notes in an arc at the bottom right (Hot Stones biggest, under the thumb), with a gold
  cooldown ring and a pop when ready; relics as small round chips. No parchment band over the board.
- Damage numbers, "CRIT!" on a muddied raider, a short hit-stop on Hot Stones, a shake when a building falls, "+1" and
  a "driven off" tally. Nobody dies: raiders are driven off.
- Tips wait for a breather and sit at the bottom, never over the fight. The scouting lines moved to a "Who's coming"
  card at Dusk.

### A Night that needs you
- A wave waits until the last is cleared (40 seconds at most), then a 15-second breather.
- In the breather: three boon notes, pick one for the next wave ("Duct-tape the chute", "Bluetooth at full volume",
  "Hand out umbrellas", "Hotter stones"), and a round *Call now* that brings the next wave at once for 10% more
  salvage.
- Stars are earned: one if the Hearth stands, two above 60%, three above 80% with the Night's objective ("No building
  wrecked", "The Prophet never swoons", "Three raiders in one Hot Stone"). A Night left to the towers earns one.
- The first Night teaches instead of punishing: about three minutes, one building wrecked at most.
- The Prophet's first perk comes at level 2, so the first Dawn ends on a choice.
- The Dawn card says how the stars were earned, what the Night paid, and when a perk waits.

### The first fifteen minutes
- After the Woodcutter, four nosy locals: "Meet the nosy locals" on the big button, a one-wave mini Night the Prophet
  meets alone. No shield, nothing wrecked.
- Then the Chute, and two errands with a choice (a Cottage or a Storage Hut, a Peat Cut or a Cultist Hut), the same
  reward either way. Done errands claim themselves.
- Two builders from the start.
- Towels come from fighting: one per star above the first, one per Pilgrims' stage (two for a boss). None from errands.
- The "Who's coming" card opens once by itself before the first Night.

### One world screen
- Lands (the Map) has your preachers on it: who's out, a story from the road, and three places worth a walk. It opens on
  the nearest place a preacher can reach; places out of reach are dimmed.
- The lord's hall opens from the Tension strip and from his seat on the map. The Missionaries and Tithes buttons are gone:
  eight entry points instead of twelve.
- The Tension strip is a real meter, the lord's face on the edge of the tape; it leads with what matters
  ("Shielded · 11h 59m · next: Odo's flock, south road"). The season sits under the portrait.
- Preachers, not missionaries, everywhere. The Cult sheet keeps People, Comfort and Relics; what the next Hearth level
  brings is on the Hearth's card; the Prophet sheet is about the Night.

### Smaller things
- While shielded, the big button walks the Pilgrims' Way. Side shortcuts arrive one at a time.
- The Dawn card waits for its own result (a tap during the replay could dismiss the wrong card); no speech bubbles over
  it; the camera shows the village behind it.
- The ✓ ✗ Turn buttons never cover the Hearth while placing a building.
- The server's replay check shows in development builds only.

### The look (first step of the remodel)
- At Night: a warm pool of light round the Hearth, fire baskets, torches up the raiders' road, bloom on the fire, a
  cooler and punchier grade, a soft vignette.
- Ink outlines on the Prophet, the raiders, the buildings and the towers.

## Before it goes out

1. **The server ships with this build.** It plans the Nights and replays them, so it needs the same simulation
   (rules 1, `errands-2026.11.18`, `village-2026.11.18`): deploy `main` after merging `playtest-and-redesign`
   (`deploy/deploy-prod.sh`). Against the old server a Night is planned by the old rules, the micro-raid is refused, and
   the level-2 perk is rejected.
2. **Sign the AAB with the upload key.** The build machine doesn't have it, so
   `client/CultOfComfort.Unity/Builds/Android/CultOfComfort-debugsigned.aab` is signed with the debug key. Either
   re-sign it:
   ```sh
   cp CultOfComfort-debugsigned.aab CultOfComfort-0.2.0.aab
   zip -d CultOfComfort-0.2.0.aab 'META-INF/*'
   jarsigner -sigalg SHA256withRSA -digestalg SHA-256 \
     -keystore ~/keystores/cultofcomfort-upload.jks -storepass "$(cat ~/keystores/cultofcomfort-upload.pass.txt)" \
     CultOfComfort-0.2.0.aab upload
   ```
   or rebuild it signed where the key is:
   `CULTOFCOMFORT_KEYSTORE_PASS=$(cat ~/keystores/cultofcomfort-upload.pass.txt) Unity … -executeMethod CultOfComfort.Editor.BuildScripts.BuildAndroidRelease -versionCode 2 -versionName 0.2.0`.
3. A playtest on the phone (or the emulator against the new server): C, D, E and F1 were checked in tests and screen
   captures, and C on the emulator offline.
