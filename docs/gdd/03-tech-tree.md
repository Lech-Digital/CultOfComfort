# 03 — Tech Tree: From Mud to Enlightenment

## Eras

| Era | Hearth gate | Research gate | Theme |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mud Era** | 1–3 | — | Gravity, levers, things falling on people |
| **Comfort Era** | 4–7 | "Plumbing, Probably" | Pressure, heat, running water |
| **Enlightenment Era** | 8+ | "Reading, Unfortunately" | Optics, steam, mass communication |

## Combat Vocabulary

- **Damage types:** Blunt, Wet (debuff), Heat, Blind (control), Morale.
- **Status effects:**
  - *Muddied* — cannot sprint.
  - *Soaked* — takes +50% Heat damage.
  - *Blinded* — stunned.
- **Core synergies:**
  - Slow / *Muddied* → Sapling Flinger crits on stationary targets.
  - *Soaked* → Hypocaust Vent ×1.5 damage.
